08 / FAQ

Straight answers.

Which CMS do you recommend?

Usually WordPress with a custom block theme and structured fields. It has no license cost, your staff probably already know it, SEO and tracking are handled by standard tools, and you're never locked into us.

Will moving our website affect our email?

No. We change only the website's DNS records at launch. Your email settings (MX, SPF, DKIM) and domain registration stay untouched, so inboxes never notice.

Can our non-technical staff really update the site?

Yes. Content lives in simple fields with locked layouts. You get a live training session plus a written guide and short videos for anyone who joins later.

What is GEO, and do we need it?

Generative Engine Optimization means structuring your site so AI assistants can understand, trust and cite it. More people now ask AI for recommendations, so it's becoming as important as Google rankings.

How do you handle accessibility?

We design and build to WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard navigation, ARIA labels, semantic HTML, visible focus states and high-contrast text. We test with automated tools and by hand, and deliver a report.

What happens after launch?

We stay available for design-heavy requests and larger updates, on demand or through a light care plan. Routine updates stay with your team.
